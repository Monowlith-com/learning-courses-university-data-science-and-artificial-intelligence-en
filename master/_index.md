# Course introduction – University · Data Science and Artificial Intelligence {#kurseinstieg}

## Profile and objectives {#S1}

This course contains **31 modules and 240 lessons per language**, with accompanying technical diagrams. It is designed for independent study and awards no university degree, ECTS credits or university admission rights.

The thirty-one modules cover data analysis, mathematics, programming, statistics, machine learning, symbolic AI, neural networks, research practice, a Master's bridge, probabilistic models, computer vision, graph learning, recommender systems, and MLOps.
